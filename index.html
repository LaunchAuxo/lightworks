Frequently asked questions

How much does lawn care cost in Cedar Rapids?

Every yard is different, so we quote each property individually — most quotes go out within one business day. Pricing depends on lot size, terrain, and how often you'd like service. Quotes are always free and there's no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Cedar Rapids and the surrounding towns — as far north as Robins, south to Swisher and Shueyville, west to Fairfax, and east to Marion. That includes Hiawatha, Ely, and everywhere in between. If you're unsure whether your address is in range, send a quote request and we'll let you know.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. You can book one-time work like a cleanup or mulch install, or set up recurring mowing that you can pause or cancel anytime. We'd rather earn your repeat business than lock it in.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Light Work Exterior Services is fully insured, and the owner is on-site for every job. If we ever damage something, we make it right.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out the quote form on this page, or call us at (319) 423-9063. Tell us your address and what you need done — we'll usually respond within one business day, and we can do a free walkthrough for bigger landscaping projects.
