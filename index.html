Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Give us a call — we're happy to walk through pricing, scheduling, or anything else.

How much do your services cost in Cedar Rapids?

Every job is different, so we quote each property individually — most quotes go out within one business day. Pricing depends on the service, property size, and how often you'd like it done. Quotes are always free and there's no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Cedar Rapids and the surrounding towns — as far north as Robins, south to Swisher and Shueyville, west to Fairfax, and east to Marion. That includes Hiawatha, Ely, and everywhere in between. If you're unsure whether your address is in range, send a quote request and we'll let you know.

Do I have to sign a contract?

No. You can book one-time services like power washing or a yard cleanup, or set up recurring lawn care that you can pause or cancel anytime. We'd rather earn your repeat business than lock it in.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Light Work Exterior Services is fully insured, and the owner is on-site for every job. If we ever damage something, we make it right.

How do I get a quote?
